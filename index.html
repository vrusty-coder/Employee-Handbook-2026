Leave planner

Your annual leave entitlement

  • Casual Leave (CL): 7 days
  • Sick Leave (SL): 7 days
  • Earned / Floater Leave (EL): 8 days

CL and SL lapse if not used in the calendar year. EL may be eligible for encashment as per company policy and applicable law.

Sandwich leave rule

If you take leave on both sides of a weekly off, the intervening weekly off is also counted as leave. Example: Leave on Friday and Monday → Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday all counted as leave.

How to book travel

  • All bookings must be done via Travel Desk only.
  • Email: traveldesk@gototheaddress.com
  • Include: date of travel, time, destination, duration, purpose.
  • Independent bookings are not eligible for reimbursement.

Report a concern (Grievance & POSH)

Grievance redressal – steps

  1. Discuss with your Reporting Manager (if appropriate).
  2. If unresolved or involves your manager, write to HR.
  3. If still unresolved, escalate to Senior Management.

Include: your name & ID, department, date(s), description, any evidence, and desired resolution.

POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment)

  • Complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC).
  • Time limit: within 3 months from the incident (or last incident).
  • Complaint can be sent by email to any IC member.
  • Confidentiality and protection against retaliation apply.

For exact IC member names and contacts, check the official notice or contact HR.

Mediclaim guide

  • Coverage: up to INR 2,00,000 per employee per policy year.
  • Eligibility: after probation, for permanent employees.
  • Covers: hospitalization, surgeries, diagnostics (as per policy).
  • Claims: follow insurer process; inform HR within 24 hours of hospitalization/treatment.

Coverage ends when employment ends. Company may change provider or terms with notice.
